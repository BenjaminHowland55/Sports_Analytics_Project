Source

Where the data comes from

All data on this site comes from nflverse, a free, open-source project that collects and publishes NFL data. We download it with nflreadpy, the official nflverse package for Python.

Datasets used

Play-by-play (load_pbp)
One row per play, including EPA (Expected Points Added). This is the source of every defensive-efficiency number on the site.
Participation (load_participation)
Play-level charting: whether the quarterback was pressured, how many players rushed, man or zone coverage, and which defenders were on the field.

How it gets to this site

  1. The script load_to_database.py history downloads every season from 2000 to the current one with nflreadpy.
  2. It keeps regular-season run and pass plays only, leaving out playoffs, special teams, and plays wiped out by penalties.
  3. It matches each play to its charting row (2016 on) and totals everything into one row per defense per season.
  4. It uploads the result to our database. The Stats page reads Arizona's rows from the team_defense_history table.

What the Stats columns mean

season, team
The regular season and the defense's team abbreviation.
plays
Run and pass plays the defense faced.
epa_per_play_allowed
Average EPA the defense allowed per play. Lower is better; 0 is about league average.
pressure_rate
Share of opponent dropbacks where the quarterback was pressured (hurried, hit, or sacked).
blitz_rate
Share of opponent dropbacks with 5 or more pass rushers.
man_coverage_rate
Share of charted pass plays where the defense played man coverage instead of zone.

Limits

  • Pressure and blitz rates start in 2016, and man coverage starts in 2018. About 90% of pass plays are charted from 2016 to 2022, and all of them from 2023 on. Rates use charted plays only.
  • How plays were charted changed over the years (for example, league-wide pressure rates jump between 2017 and 2018), so compare rates across eras with care.
  • The current season only has efficiency numbers so far. Its charting data hasn't been published by nflverse yet.
  • Pressure and coverage are recorded by people watching game film, so they involve some judgment.
